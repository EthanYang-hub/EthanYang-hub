# Discount Analytics and Task Dispatch Platform

## Company

SF Technology

## Role

Java Backend Engineer

## Period

2022

## Project Summary

Built and maintained a business analytics and task workflow platform used for discount-related customer analysis, abnormal-customer monitoring, reporting, and operational follow-up.

## Business Context

The platform was built as an internal system for discount-related business operations. It helped business teams analyze customer pricing and profitability by month, identify risky or underperforming cases, and assign follow-up tasks to regional staff for handling.

## My Responsibilities

- Participated in initial project setup and schema design
- Wrote Hive SQL for large-scale reporting logic
- Developed backend APIs and business modules
- Implemented frontend pages and end-to-end data integration
- Supported reporting, task dispatch, and customer analysis workflows

## Tech Stack

Vue 3, Element Plus, Spring Boot, MyBatis, MySQL, Hive SQL

## Challenges

The main challenge was turning large amounts of business data into practical operational workflows. The system needed to connect analytics, reporting, and task assignment in a way that sales and regional operation teams could actually use day to day.

## What I Did

I worked across the full implementation flow, including database design, reporting logic, backend APIs, frontend pages, and data handoff between modules. I helped structure the platform so that customer analysis results could directly drive follow-up actions instead of remaining as static reports.

## Outcome

- Reduced manual reporting effort by roughly 1,000 hours
- Improved visibility into discount-related customer performance
- Supported task-based follow-up for abnormal or low-profitability customer cases

This project demonstrated my ability to build internal business systems that connect data analysis with real operational action.
